### Which boundary built the suitability mask

- `gis_analysis.build_criteria_stack` reads `os.path.join(data_dir, 'boundary.geojson')` (gis_analysis.py:703) with `data_dir='data'`, i.e. **the same file**, `data/boundary.geojson` (SHA-256 `2d3dde7851f00e47…`). There is no other boundary input.
- It is rasterised with `all_touched=True` (gis_analysis.py:158), so the mask includes every cell the polygon touches. Mask cells: 123,555 (expected 123,555: **True**). The mask therefore extends up to one cell (~0.3 km) beyond the polygon edge, which is why **214** of the 639 'outside' points still fall in a masked cell and would receive a suitability score.
